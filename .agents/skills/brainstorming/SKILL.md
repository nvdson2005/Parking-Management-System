# Brainstorming

1. Review the request, related designs in `docs/superpowers/specs/`, and relevant project conventions. Carry forward decisions the user has already made.
2. Clarify unresolved choices that materially affect scope or behavior. When there are meaningful alternatives, explain their tradeoffs and recommend one.
3. Agree on the proposed design with the user. Cover the goal, scope, expected behavior, and acceptance criteria. Include data, permissions, failure cases, and verification where relevant.
4. Record the agreed design under `docs/superpowers/specs/` and follow the Spec Kit sequence in `AGENTS.md`. Use the approved design as input to `speckit-specify`.
